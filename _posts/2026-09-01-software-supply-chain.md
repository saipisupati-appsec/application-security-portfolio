Dependency scanning is important, but it is only one part of a defensible software supply chain. Teams also need a dependable view of what they build, where packages come from, and which artifacts reach production.

## Evidence improves response

An SBOM, trusted package policies, and build provenance make incident response faster because teams can identify affected artifacts and their owners without reconstructing the supply chain under pressure.
