The purpose of threat modeling is not a perfect diagram. It is a shared understanding of what needs protection, where trust changes, and which mitigations must be present before release.

## Keep the output actionable

Link each material threat to a design decision, requirement, test, or accepted residual risk. This creates a useful trail from architecture review to delivery.
