## Approach

Start from assets, trust boundaries, data flows, and attacker goals. Use STRIDE to identify abuse cases, then record mitigations that a team can build and test.

## Deliverables

- Data-flow diagrams and trust boundaries
- Threat and mitigation register
- Security requirements and acceptance criteria
- Residual-risk decisions with accountable owners
