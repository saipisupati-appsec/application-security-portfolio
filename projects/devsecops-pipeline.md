## Objective

Integrate security checks into normal delivery workflows so developers receive actionable feedback before release.

## Controls

- Secret scanning and push protection
- SAST and dependency scanning
- Container and infrastructure-as-code scanning
- SBOM generation and artifact traceability
- Policy-based gates with documented exception handling

## Outcome

The target operating model treats pipeline findings as engineering work: visible, owned, prioritized, and verifiable.
